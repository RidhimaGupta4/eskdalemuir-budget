Eskdalemuir budget explorer

Wind turbines within 50 km of the Eskdalemuir seismic array share a vibration budget of 0.336 nanometres. This page runs the UK government's published 2026 calculation in your browser, shows where the budget goes, and lets you test what each layer of caution costs.

What the budget meant, 2005 to 2026

The limit has stayed at 0.336 nm. Only the 2005 version stated how often it could be exceeded.

2005
Keele report (Styles et al.)

The limit is the median of 330 noisy-day recordings. Wind farms may add as much noise as the array already hears. We accept that for 20 % of the time averaged over a year, seismic amplitude levels may exceed the permitted threshold.

2014
New algorithm (Xi Engineering)

Utilisation re-estimated at about 0.9 of amplitude, then set to 1 until, and if, further work is conducted in this area.

2020
Working Group report (SGV-202)

Described as a now defunct utilisation factor, set to one for simplicity.

2026
DESNZ consultation

Six fitted manufacturer models, every turbine at the 12 m/s reference at once. No exceedance probability stated.

About this page

Built from the repository's computed outputs by scripts/07_build_dashboard.py. No parameter is fitted or adjusted to match a published figure; published values are comparison targets only. The engine badge at the top compares this page's calculation with the Python reference on every load.
